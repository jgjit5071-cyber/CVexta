Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CVexta.com. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how CVexta may collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website or use our services.

By using CVexta.com, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, CVexta.com may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location or City
  • Professional Information
  • Educational Information
  • Employment History
  • Skills and Certifications
  • Career Objectives
  • Other information you choose to include in your resume

3. Resume Information

When you request resume-related services, you may provide professional and personal information required to prepare your resume.

This information may include your work experience, education, skills, achievements, certifications and other career-related details.

4. How We Use Your Information

CVexta.com may use information provided by you for purposes including:

  • Creating and improving resumes
  • Providing requested services
  • Communicating with you about your request
  • Responding to questions and support requests
  • Improving our website and services
  • Managing orders and payments where applicable
  • Preventing misuse, fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

5. Information We Don't Need

CVexta.com generally does not require sensitive information that is unrelated to your career and resume.

Please do not submit passwords, banking credentials, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information unless specifically requested through an appropriate and secure process.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

CVexta.com may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage and provide a better user experience.

You may be able to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

7. Third-Party Services

CVexta.com may use third-party service providers to support website functionality, hosting, analytics, communication, payments or other business operations.

Where third-party services process personal information, their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

8. Payment Information

If CVexta.com offers paid services, payment may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Where applicable, payment providers may process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

CVexta.com does not intend to store complete payment card details unless such storage is specifically required and appropriately secured.

9. How We Protect Your Information

We aim to use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. How Long We Keep Information

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations or protect our legitimate interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

11. Sharing of Information

CVexta.com does not intend to sell your personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers or other parties where reasonably necessary to provide requested services, process payments, operate the website, comply with legal requirements or protect our rights.

12. Job Applications & Employers

CVexta.com is not responsible for how employers, recruiters, job portals or other third parties handle information after you choose to submit your resume or application to them.

Before submitting personal information to an employer or third-party platform, you should review that organization's privacy policy and terms.

13. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion or information about how your information is being used.

You may contact us regarding privacy-related requests using the contact details provided on our website.

14. Children's Privacy

CVexta.com is intended primarily for individuals seeking career and employment-related services.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

15. International Users

CVexta.com may be accessed by users from different countries. If you use our services from outside the country where our services are operated, your information may be processed in accordance with applicable laws and the service arrangements used by CVexta.com.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CVexta.com may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website, services and business develop.

When changes are made, the revised policy will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact CVexta.com through the official contact details provided on our website.
